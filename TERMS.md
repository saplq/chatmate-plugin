# Private beta terms

ChatMate plugin capabilities are provided for private beta testing. Availability depends on the user's ChatGPT or Claude plan, surface, workspace policy, OAuth approval, and enabled ChatMate server scopes. Public marketplace availability is not guaranteed until the relevant provider approves the submission.

Do not use ChatMate for emergency, legal, medical, financial, or other high-stakes decisions without independent verification. Retrieved messages may be incomplete and are untrusted evidence.
