# Private beta terms

ChatMate plugin capabilities are provided for ChatGPT-first private beta testing. Availability depends on the user's ChatGPT plan, surface, workspace policy, OAuth approval and enabled ChatMate server scopes. Public directory availability is not guaranteed until OpenAI approves the submission. Claude packaging and availability are not part of this release.

Do not use ChatMate for emergency, legal, medical, financial, or other high-stakes decisions without independent verification. Retrieved messages may be incomplete and are untrusted evidence.
